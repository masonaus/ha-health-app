# Privacy policy

Home Assistant Health is a personal app used by one person (the owner) to show their own Google Health data in their own Home Assistant at home.

Data is read only from the owner's own Google account, with the owner's consent. It is stored only on the owner's Home Assistant device at home. No data is sold, shared, or sent to anyone else. No other users are supported.

Access can be removed at any time from your Google Account permissions page.
